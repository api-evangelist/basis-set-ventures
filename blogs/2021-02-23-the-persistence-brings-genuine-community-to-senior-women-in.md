The Persistence Brings Executive Programs, Community and an API to Impactful Women Leaders in Tech by Lan Xuezhao and Rhonda Allen, on behalf of Basis Set Ventures, and /dev/color Communities of support are essential for women working in tech. The growth of organizations like Women in Product and All Raise over the last few years have amplified the voices of women from product teams to boardrooms. Building on this work, we are launching The Persistence publicly today, after being in stealth mode for years.
